Twelve baskets left over

After five thousand people had eaten their fill, Jesus told his disciples to gather the pieces that were left over. Sunday sermons are like that. A message prepared in prayer mostly stays in the room when the service ends, and by the next week it is often forgotten — even though somewhere inside it is the one sentence that needs to be heard again, the one passage that could lift someone up.

Namun Jogak (“the remaining pieces”) is not a tool for making new messages. It gathers up the Word that has already been shared so that none of it is wasted, and puts it back into the hands of your people and neighbors all week long.

Step 1 · Add video

Drag in your sermon video

Drag a sermon video file your church owns onto the window, or choose it, and the app transcribes it right away. A full service recording is fine. Or just tap a video under “Recent videos” from your Downloads and Movies folders.

Choose what to make, too — six 60-second Shorts, a 1, 2 or 3-minute summary of the whole sermon, or a non-sermon video (testimony, lecture).

Step 2 · Pick a clip

Moments of grace, already finished

It picks non-overlapping moments worth sharing again and shows six finished Shorts with title, captions, church template and music. Play each card and switch its mood (warm · bold · calm · majestic).

Each clip starts at the beginning of a sentence and ends on the line that closes the thought. Like it? “Use this”. Want to adjust? “Edit”.

Step 3 · Polish & finish

Fix things in place, with a few buttons

Tap the title or a caption in the preview to fix it, and drag it to move it. One mood card changes template, font, music and caption motion together; “one sentence more / less” sets the length to where the Word lands.

56 templates · 11 title fonts · 41 music tracks including hymn piano accompaniments. Your church logo and the sermon passage go in automatically.

Step 3 · Trim panel

Cut filler and repeats by lifting them out

Mark what to remove on the waveform and words, then drag it up to cut it. “Find filler · repeats (AI)” marks long pauses and repeated words for you. Tap a seam to bring a cut back; cut words disappear from the captions too.

Step 3 · Finish 1–5

Top to bottom — finished steps get a ✓

Choose a thumbnail → caption file (Korean · English SRT) → save video (1080×1920 vertical) → upload to YouTube (now or scheduled) → share on KakaoTalk / bulletin. Skip any step you do not need.

If YouTube is connected, you confirm the channel name before uploading. You can also schedule a whole week — one Short a day at the time you choose.

New · KakaoTalk / bulletin share card

One picture and a message for your members

So members can open the Short right away, it makes a share card with the title, closing line and a QR code, in 52 weekly church-year colors and 6 frames, and writes the message to send with it.

Copy the picture into a KakaoTalk chat or save it for the bulletin. The app never sends anything to KakaoTalk by itself.

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find your answer? Ask on our Q&A board and read other churches’ questions.

Is my sermon video sent anywhere?

No. Transcription and rendering both happen on your Mac, PC or iPad. Only when you use AI clip suggestions is the transcript text sent to the AI you choose (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini).

Does it work with English sermons?

Yes. Transcription works on the device in English, Korean and other major languages (Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German) — choose the speech language in Settings. The app screens are available in English and Korean.

Do I need an AI account or API key?

No. By default you paste a request into the AI chat you already use, so a free account works. There is also a manual mode where you choose the clip yourself without AI.

Can I use worship backing tracks or popular songs?

Copyrighted music can get your Short muted on YouTube or its revenue claimed. All tracks in the app were made by us, so they are safe to use. For trending songs, use “Add sound” in the YouTube app.

What if connecting to YouTube doesn’t work?

To connect — in Settings → YouTube, click Connect. Sign in with the Google account that owns your church channel and click Allow on the permission screen. Namun Jogak has completed Google’s app verification (October 2026).

If uploads fail after connecting — make sure all checkboxes were ticked on the permission screen, then disconnect and connect again in Settings. A Google account without a YouTube channel needs to create a channel first.

If you’d rather not connect — you don’t have to. Upload the Short file yourself in the YouTube app or YouTube Studio, and copy the title, description and hashtags the app wrote.

My uploads go up as “Private”.

Apart from Google’s app verification, YouTube separately reviews an app’s use of YouTube. Until that review finishes, videos uploaded by the app may be set to private. The app tells you when this happens — switch them to public in YouTube Studio. After the review, public and scheduled publishing work as set.

Does my work keep taking up storage?

No. Audio files and transcripts created while working are deleted after 7 days of not being used (you can change this to 3, 14 or 30 days, or turn it off). Your original video files and finished Shorts are never deleted.

Can I start from a YouTube link?

No. Since version 0.9, you start from a video file. Under YouTube’s policies, an app that connects to YouTube (for uploading) cannot also download YouTube videos. For videos on your own channel, download them from YouTube Studio (Content → ⋮ → Download).

Can I use it for church events or Sunday school videos?

Yes. Templates for Sunday previews, Friday prayer, worship lyrics, Sunday school, youth, quiet time, discipleship and retreats are included, and you can rename the labels to your own ministries. For testimonies, lectures or interviews, choose 6 general clips and AI picks the most moving moments without sermon rules.

About the YouTube connection

To use “Upload to YouTube”, you sign in with your own Google account and grant permission. The app uses this permission only to upload the Short you select to your own channel, with the title, description, privacy setting and optional scheduled publish time you enter, and to show the channel name it will upload to. Tokens are stored only in your device’s secure credential store (Keychain on macOS/iPadOS, Credential Manager on Windows) and are never sent to our servers. By using this feature you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy also applies. See our Privacy Policy. You can disconnect at any time in the app’s Settings.
